Zenta Engineering is an educational website for engineering students, diploma students, graduate engineers and working professionals. We explain engineering concepts from first principles and connect them to the equipment and practice found in real plants, sites and products.

## What we publish

- **Articles** on fundamentals, working principles, equipment, comparisons and troubleshooting across 20 engineering disciplines.
- **Tutorials and guides** that take a subject step by step, lesson by lesson.
- **Interview questions** with full answers and practice MCQs.
- **Engineering projects** for diploma and degree students.
- **Resources** such as study notes, reference guides and standards references.

## How we work

Every technical item is written against reliable references and checked by our technical team before it is published. Our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) explains how content is written, reviewed, corrected and updated.

Electronics Engineering articles are written by [Santaji Gadepatil](/author/santaji-gadepatil/), B.E. Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering. Other articles are written by the [Zenta Engineering editorial staff](/author/editorial-staff/).
