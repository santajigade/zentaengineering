Zenta Engineering publishes educational content for engineering students and working engineers. This page explains how that content is written, checked and kept up to date.

## Accuracy comes first

- Every technical claim must be supported by a reliable reference: an engineering textbook, a published standard, manufacturer documentation, a government or university source, or a peer-reviewed paper.
- References are listed at the end of each article. Standards are cited by number, title and edition year.
- We do not publish invented figures, test results, case studies or quotes. If a value cannot be verified, it is left out.
- Where a value depends on a standard, a region or an assumption, the article says so.

## Calculations and worked examples

- Every worked example states what is given, what is found, the assumptions made, the units used and the limits of the method.
- SI units are used first. Other units are added where a topic is region specific.
- Where a calculation can be checked with a calculator tool, we link to it.

## Technical review

- Each item is checked by a reviewer with relevant engineering knowledge before it goes live. The reviewer's name and the review date are shown on the page.
- Content without a completed review is not published.

## How content is prepared

- Topics are chosen to explain engineering concepts clearly, not to repeat what already exists elsewhere.
- Drafts may be prepared with the help of software tools, including AI writing assistants. No draft is published until a human reviewer has checked every fact against the cited references.

## Updates and corrections

- Articles are reviewed when a cited standard is revised or when a reader reports an error.
- The "Last updated" date changes only when content is meaningfully changed, not for small wording edits.
- To report an error, email <a href="mailto:contact@zentaengineering.com">contact@zentaengineering.com</a> with the page link and the correction. Confirmed errors are fixed promptly.

## Independence, advertising and links

- Advertising, when present, is clearly separated from content and never placed inside worked examples, tables or equations.
- Sponsored content, if ever published, will be labelled "Sponsored" and follows this same policy.
- Affiliate and sponsored links are marked, and products are mentioned only where they are relevant to the topic.
- We link to other websites, including our sister educational sites, only where the link genuinely helps the reader.

## Safety

Engineering work on electrical, pressure, chemical, lifting and rotating equipment can be dangerous. Our content is educational. Always follow applicable codes, site procedures and the advice of a qualified, authorised person.
