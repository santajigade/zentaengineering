This policy explains what information zentaengineering.com ("Zenta Engineering", "we") collects and how it is used.

## Information we collect

- **Information you send us.** If you email us or subscribe to our newsletter, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.
- **Usage information.** We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider records standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

## Advertising and cookies

We may show advertising served by Google and its partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/). Visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## How we use information

- To operate, maintain and improve the website.
- To reply to your messages and send newsletters you asked for. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.
- To measure which content is useful.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Embedded content

Some pages embed calculators from our sister site zentacalculator.com. Embedded content behaves as if you visited that site and is covered by its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To ask about, correct or delete information you sent us, email [contact@zentaengineering.com](mailto:contact@zentaengineering.com).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
